# Roadmap and feature parity

Status values: **done** (a named test proves it), **partial** (the gap is stated), **missing**.
Nothing is marked done without a test. Milestone scope: `docs/plan.md` (amended 2026-10-09,
DEC-028). Decisions: `docs/decisions.md`.

**Positioning.** Tenon is for makers, students and small shops who cannot afford the commercial
tools and find the free ones rough. It wins on reliability, coherence and speed, not on feature
count: when two tasks compete, the one that makes existing features more dependable and pleasant
goes first.

Current milestone: **M8 sheet metal basics**, started 2026-10-11 and reported to the owner the
same day. The owner asked for the gap with Inventor's sheet metal to be closed before going
on: the second round, Corner Seam, Cut Across Bend and Bend (the section after M8 below) are
done and reported, and wait for the owner's confirmation. Next is M9 performance.

**M8** brought:
- sheet metal parts: a rule whose sizes (thickness, bend radius, reliefs, K factor) are
  parameters of the part (DEC-057);
- Face and Flange: walls bent up from edges of the sheet at any angle, with bend reliefs beside
  narrow ones and corner reliefs where they meet (DEC-059);
- the flat pattern, worked out from the folded solid with each bend as wide as its neutral
  surface is long, checked against hand-computed bend allowances (DEC-058);
- DXF and SVG of the flat pattern for a cutter (DEC-060);
- a Sheet Metal tab laid out as Inventor's, Sheet Metal Defaults, and the flat pattern shown in
  place of the folded part (DEC-061);
- the example [examples/m8-tray](examples/m8-tray);
- in the second round: walls mitred where they meet and corner reliefs centred on the bend
  lines, as Inventor's are (DEC-063); Hem and Contour Flange (DEC-064); then Corner Seam, with
  overlapped seams and a seam, gap and relief of a corner's own (DEC-066); then Cut Across
  Bend, a profile cut round the bends as it is drawn on the flat sheet, with Project Flat
  Pattern to draw it on (DEC-068); then Bend, which joins two faces made apart, with the four
  kinds of double bend for faces in parallel planes (DEC-070).

M7 (standard parts and maker workflow) was confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-11 (DEC-054), who
asked again for the interface to come closer to Inventor's; the sketch environment was gone
through first (the section after M7 below).

**The sketch environment's second round** (DEC-055, DEC-056) brought:
- Project Geometry: edges of the part shown in a sketch, followed when the part changes, and a
  new sketch on a face starting with the face's edges;
- centre lines, diameters dimensioned from them, and Revolve taking one as its axis;
- constraint symbols as pictures that can be selected and deleted, and dimension values that
  lie along their lines;
- Move, Copy, Rotate, Scale, Extend, Split, and rectangular and circular sketch patterns;
- slots, rectangles about a centre and by three points, arcs by centre and tangent.

**M7** brought:
- a standard parts library of ten families and 212 sizes (ISO metric screws, nuts and washers,
  ball bearings, tube, angle, flat bar and a generic T-slot profile), each an ordinary part with
  a design table, built from sizes typed in as facts (DEC-049);
- standard parts placed in an assembly in a size, from one file per family kept beside the
  assembly, and parts started from them;
- a 3D print check: watertight, overhangs, thinnest wall, fits the bed, with what it finds
  tinted in the model (DEC-051);
- 3MF export for parts and assemblies, a hardened STL export, and written meshes that keep
  their tolerance everywhere (DEC-052);
- dimensions of one sketch that change together are set together (DEC-050);
- the example [examples/m7-end-cap](examples/m7-end-cap).

**M6 part feature breadth** was confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-10 (DEC-046). It brought:
- sweep, coil and loft;
- draft, split and combine;
- threads, cosmetic or cut into the part, and tapered extrusions;
- materials, appearance and physical properties in a Properties window (DEC-043), carried into
  assemblies and the parts list;
- design tables: one part in several sizes (DEC-044);
- at the owner's request: revolve fixed for profiles across the axis (DEC-039), sketch
  dimensions drawn and placed as dimensions (DEC-041), and a first round of closer matching to
  Inventor's workflow, tracked in [docs/inventor-fidelity.md](docs/inventor-fidelity.md)
  (DEC-042);
- the example [examples/m6-fittings](examples/m6-fittings);
- after the owner handed over the decisions left open in the M6 report: sketch curves that
  cross divide a profile into regions that are clicked apart (DEC-046), driven dimensions
  (DEC-047), and assembly components in a named size of their part (DEC-048).

M5 reliability and project format was confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-09.
